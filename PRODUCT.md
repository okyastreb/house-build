# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

One person — the owner — planning to buy land and build a capsule/modular house in Portugal as a permanent resident. Ukrainian speaker. Uses this as a personal reference guide throughout a multi-year process.

## Product Purpose

A personal reference guide covering the full process of buying land and building a house in Portugal: land types, legal checks, permits, utilities, team, and budget. Answers the question "what do I need to do and in what order?"

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, practical. Like a knowledgeable friend who has done this before, not a corporate consultant.

## Anti-references

- Flashy real estate marketing sites (too salesy)
- Government portal aesthetics (too bureaucratic)
- Over-designed portfolio sites (form over function)

## Design Principles

1. Information first — content is the product, decoration serves it
2. Scannable over beautiful — the user needs to find things fast
3. Calm confidence — no urgency, no hype, just reliable structure

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Comfortable reading in Ukrainian (longer words, different rhythm than English). No specific known needs.
